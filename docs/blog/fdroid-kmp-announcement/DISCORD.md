## SDAI 1.1.1 is now on F-Droid

The new KMP era of SDAI has reached F-Droid.

Version **1.1.1 (208)** is the first release from SDAI's Kotlin Multiplatform line to become available through F-Droid. It brings the Android FOSS listing forward from the old 0.6.8 generation to the modern shared foundation behind SDAI on Android and iPhone.

### What to know

- **Suggested release:** SDAI 1.1.1 (208)
- **Architecture:** `arm64-v8a` only
- **Minimum OS:** Android 7.0 or newer
- **Distribution:** official F-Droid listing and F-Droid client updates
- **Principles:** open source, no ads, no telemetry, and no provider lock-in

The arm64-only package is intentional for this FOSS release. Devices limited to `armeabi-v7a`, `x86`, or `x86_64` are not compatible with this build.

Install or update through F-Droid:
https://f-droid.org/packages/com.shifthackz.aisdv1.app.foss/

Welcome to the KMP era of SDAI on F-Droid.
