The public SDAI beta is open to iPhone users through TestFlight. You can try changes before they reach the App Store, whether that's a provider fix you've been waiting for or an update to a part of the app you use regularly.

## Join on your iPhone

1. Install Apple's TestFlight app from the App Store.
2. Open the [SDAI TestFlight page](/testflight.html) on your iPhone and follow the public invite.
3. Accept the invitation in TestFlight and install the available SDAI build.

TestFlight shows which version you're testing and when the build expires. It also shows newer builds as they're made available. See [Apple's TestFlight guide](https://testflight.apple.com/) for help with invitations, updates, and feedback.

## Give feedback from a real session

Pick something you'd do anyway, like connecting to your usual provider, generating an image, or reopening a result from the gallery. If you're testing a specific fix, repeat the steps that used to trigger the issue. That gives us something concrete to check when a change behaves differently across iPhones.

You can send feedback through TestFlight. Include your iPhone model, the iOS and beta build versions, and the steps that led to the problem. A screenshot helps when the issue is visible on screen. For generation issues, the provider name and whether you were working in text-to-image or image-to-image can narrow down the cause.

Beta builds can contain unfinished features or new bugs. For everyday generation, use the [stable SDAI release from the App Store](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/sdai-ai-image-generator/id6778314183).
